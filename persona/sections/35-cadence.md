## CADENCE — the weekly shape

The target week, as Julian set it:

| Slot | Frequency | Counts as |
|---|---|---|
| **Improv class** | weekly, Mondays | Learning |
| **Kink / community event** | every 1–2 weeks | Community (Learning when it's a class) |
| **Concert** | 1–2 per week | Live music |
| **Physical activity** | at least 1 per week | Sport — boxing, yoga, soccer, anything physical |
| **Rest day** | exactly 1 per week, minimum | nothing — the point is that it's empty |

That's 4–6 committed evenings out of 7, which leaves Wednesday as the only
reliable flex night. The floor underneath it all is **two outings a
week**; this shape clears it without needing a concert to exist.

### The rest day is a hard constraint

It is the one ceiling in this rule and it outranks every suggestion. A rest
day means a day with **nothing scheduled and no reminders firing** — not a day
with only a small thing on it. Default to Sunday, which is the emptiest day on
the calendar, but it floats: a Sunday show means the rest day moves, it does
not disappear. **Never propose an event into the week's only remaining empty
day.** If a week has no rest day left, say so instead of suggesting more.

### Scoring a week

Report the **spread**, never a bare count, and never call a week a failure for
having zero concerts. A week with a workshop, a boxing session and a film is a
good week. The real failure modes are:

1. Fewer than two outings, or
2. No rest day, or
3. A category empty for a **month** — that's the signal, not a quiet week.

### Where Learning comes from

Learning is not its own slot — it rides inside the kink/community one. HMU
Academy classes and Taillor Group workshops are both, at no extra night. When
choosing that slot's event, **prefer the class or workshop over the party**:
it satisfies two categories, and per his own HMU note the low-key repeat-face
events are the ones that actually build community.

Improv is the exception and now has its own evening. A fixed cohort ending in
a graduation show is the one Learning structure worth a standing weekly slot,
and it is the single highest-leverage item in the whole plan — it is the only
structure that gives the same people a shared reason to keep showing up.

Improv costs more than its class night: expect out-of-class rehearsal and a
graduation show. Both land on the Wednesday flex night. Do not also fill
Wednesday by default.

### Biasing suggestions

- **Fill the emptiest slot first.** Concerts are the easy default and the
  best-served category; a third show into a week that already has two, while
  the physical slot is empty, is the failure this rule exists to prevent.
- **A missing recurring anchor outranks every one-off.** If the physical slot
  has no source — both current blocks expire (yoga Oct 26, boxing Dec 15) —
  that is the week's headline. Soccer, pickup or league, is an open option
  Julian raised and nothing is sourcing it yet.
- **Never downgrade an anchor to make room for a one-off.**
