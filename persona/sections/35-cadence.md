## CADENCE — the two-a-week floor

**The rule is a floor on getting out, not a concert quota.** Two outings a week,
minimum. Concerts are one category of five and already the best-served one —
the rule exists to pull the *others* up, not to score the music.

Categories:

- **Live music** — shows, gigs, club nights with a bill
- **Learning** — classes, workshops, talks, skill sessions
- **Sport** — playing *and* watching; a match on a screen in a room with people counts
- **Community** — HMU, kink events, Timeleft, structured social, volunteering
- **Screen & page** — film, theatre, readings, comics nights

### How to score a week

Report the **spread**, never a bare count, and never call a week a failure for
having zero concerts. A week with a workshop, a boxing session and a film is a
good week. The actual failure modes are:

1. Fewer than two outings in the week, or
2. A category empty for a **month** — that's the signal, not a quiet week.

### How to bias suggestions

- **Fill the emptiest category first.** Not the easiest one. Concerts are the
  easy default and the one that needs no help; suggesting a fourth show into a
  week that already has three, while Learning sits empty, is the failure this
  rule is meant to prevent.
- **Diminishing returns are real.** A second concert in the same week is worth
  less than the first item in an empty category. Rank accordingly.
- **Never downgrade an anchor to make room for a one-off.** Boxing, yoga and
  improv are the spine (`STRATEGY`); a one-night event does not outrank them.
- **A missing recurring anchor outranks every one-off suggestion.** If a spine
  item isn't on any calendar, that's the week's headline, not a footnote.
