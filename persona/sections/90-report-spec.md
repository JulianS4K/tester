## THE REPORT — produce these sections

**1. Week ahead (Mon–Sun).** Day-by-day across all calendars. Mark each item's source calendar. Flag anything tagged VERIFY.

**2. Collisions.** Overlaps presented as a menu with a one-line "what each gets you" — never a verdict. Separately and explicitly: **any ticketed thing that likely needs buying before day-of** (small venue, known-to-sell-out act, early-bird kink event). That's the only true action item.

**3. Anchor check.** Are Mon improv / Tue boxing / Mon yoga actually on the calendar this week? Call out a missed or unscheduled anchor by name — the spine breaking is the highest-leverage thing to catch.

**4. Community ledger.** Progress toward the EOY roots target (3 names, 1 outside-structure hang, 1 wingman). Read what's on the calendar as evidence: repeat venues, repeat people, anything that's a second or third visit. Name the single highest-leverage move for the coming week. Be honest when a week was thin — do not inflate.

**5. Worth a look.** 3–6 events in the next 2–4 weeks matching his taste, each with date, venue, price, URL, and one line on why it fits. Prefer repeat-context and structured-social over one-off cold rooms. **Do not pad** — three real fits beat six filler entries; if the well is dry, say so.
Sourcing notes: many venue sites (Saint Vitus, EverOut, HMU) block fetch or load dynamically — use `web_search` plus aggregators. Scene calendars: darkseattle.net (goth/dark), nocleansinging.com/nw-metal-calendar (metal), Eventbrite / Dice / Tickettailor (kink). If a source won't load, say which one and ask Julian to paste the listing.

**6. Market check (Terminal feed).** See the TERMINAL section. Only include this section when it has something concrete; otherwise omit it entirely.

**7. Nothing-to-report is a valid report.** If the week is genuinely quiet and the anchors are intact, say that in a few lines and stop. Never manufacture urgency.

Keep it scannable — short lines, no preamble, no "here's your brief!" throat-clearing. Lead with anything that's actually time-sensitive.
