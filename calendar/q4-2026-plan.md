## The three problems

### 1. Three of the four EOY structures were never scheduled

The EOY target event names four structures and says all are "live by end of
Sep". Checked against the calendars:

- Boxing, Tuesdays — **live**
- Improv Level 2, Mondays at Magnet, "from Sep 28" — **on no calendar**
- Vinyl Night, 2nd Wednesdays at Sycamore — **on no calendar**
- Neighborhood regular, the Cortelyou block — **on no calendar** (only a
  biweekly reminder to check a website, which is not an outing)

Improv is the costly one. The EOY note calls the graduation show "your single
best shot" because a cohort that ends in a shared performance is the one
structure with a built-in reason for people to keep seeing each other. It is
also the only Learning item in the plan. Its absence is why Learning is empty.

The Nov 9 mid-point check asks "is there anyone in the improv cohort you click
with?" — as scheduled, it will ask about a cohort that does not exist.

### 2. Monday evening is double-booked by design

Yoga Foundations is Mondays 7:30pm at Studio Umi on Cortelyou. Magnet's Level 2
runs Monday evenings in Manhattan. Both cannot happen. This is the likely
reason improv never got scheduled — and it was never surfaced as a choice.

This needs a decision, not a default. Either move yoga to another Studio Umi
slot, or take Level 2 on a different night or term. **VERIFY** both schedules
before committing.

### 3. The quarter ends with no anchors at all

Yoga stops Oct 26. Boxing stops Dec 15. From Dec 16 there is nothing recurring
on any calendar — including the week of the Dec 20 EOY checkpoint, which will
be reviewing a spine that has already dissolved.

Both are renewable, not finished: yoga is a block that simply was not extended,
and the boxing description frames the 12 weeks as "toward readiness for the
next level", so there is a next-level ask to make.
